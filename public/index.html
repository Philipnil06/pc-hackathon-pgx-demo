Datum PGx Pharmacogenomic Decision Support
Demo environment · synthetic patients MP Pharmacogenomics service

Choose and start an antidepressant with less guesswork

Datum is a clinical decision support that brings three things together in one view: the patient's symptom picture, their current medications and interaction risks, and their pharmacogenetics. It does not pick the drug for you. It narrows the field and flags the risks, so the choice is safer and easier to defend.

Symptom profile → current medications → genetics as a safety filter → one short, sourced recommendation. Advisory only. It does not diagnose or prescribe.

Safety guardrails

  • No diagnosis or prescribing is performed by this tool.
